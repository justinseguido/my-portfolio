FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is GoHighLevel?+

GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one marketing and CRM platform built for agencies and service businesses. It combines website hosting, landing pages, sales funnels, email/SMS marketing, appointment booking, pipeline management, and workflow automation into a single tool — replacing the need for 5–10 separate subscriptions.

How long does a typical project take?+

Most projects are delivered within 3–10 days depending on scope. A single landing page or funnel can be ready in 2–3 days. Full CRM automation builds with multiple workflows typically take 7–10 days. I'll give you a clear timeline during our discovery call.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?+

Yes — the majority of my clients are in the US, Canada, and Australia. I work async-first, so time zones are never an issue. I deliver on schedule, communicate through Slack, email, or Loom, and I'm available during overlapping hours when needed.

What industries do you specialize in?+

I specialize in real estate and medspa businesses, but I also work with service-based businesses like vacation rentals, coaching practices, and local home services. If your business relies on lead generation and appointment booking, I can help.

What's included in the free GHL audit call?+

On a free 30-minute call, I'll review your current GoHighLevel setup — workflows, pipelines, funnels, and automations — and identify gaps that could be costing you leads. You'll walk away with actionable recommendations you can implement right away, plus a clear picture of what a fully optimized system looks like for your niche.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?+

Yes. After launch I provide a handoff with full documentation and a walkthrough so you or your team can manage everything independently. If you need ongoing optimization, I offer monthly retainer packages for continued support, updates, and new automation builds.
