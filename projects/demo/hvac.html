The Real Cost of Skipping Maintenance

Small problems don't stay small.

Most HVAC failures are avoidable. Without a maintenance plan, wear and tear quietly builds until your system quits — usually on the coldest night or hottest afternoon of the year.

Surprise Breakdowns

Emergency no-heat and no-cool repairs routinely run $600–$2,500 — and always seem to happen at the worst possible moment, at premium after-hours rates.

Higher Energy Bills

A neglected system works harder and burns more energy. A dirty coil or clogged filter can add 20–30% to your monthly heating and cooling costs.

Voided Warranties

Most manufacturers require documented annual maintenance. Skip it and a $3,000+ compressor or heat-exchanger claim can be denied when you need it most.

A maintenance plan turns unpredictable emergencies into a small, planned expense.
