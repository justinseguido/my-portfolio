How we estimated this

The monthly figure is an estimate, not a measurement. It assumes 30 new patient enquiries a month and £800 value per new patient. It assumes about 50% of enquiries would normally book. Each problem found adds an assumed share of those lost (for example 25% when an enquiry gets no reply, 4% when there is no online booking), capped at 30%. Here the total came to 30%.

Website checks were run automatically on the homepage. The test enquiry, phone call and review comparison were done by hand.
