Dental Clinics

GoHighLevel CRM and automation for dental clinics

GHL Certified Admin · One-time price, you own the build

I build GoHighLevel systems for dental and cosmetic dental practices. Ad leads get followed up automatically, patients get confirmations and reminders on WhatsApp, SMS or email, and your practice software stays in sync with the CRM.

What I build for dental practices

  • CRM pipeline that shows which enquiries have booked, which are stale and which have gone cold.
  • Lead follow-up for Meta and Google ad leads, including reactivating old lead lists.
  • WhatsApp automation through the Meta Business API, with approved message templates.
  • Practice software integration: bookings, completions, cancellations and no-shows flow into GHL through Make.com or the API.
  • Treatment landing pages for high-value services like composite bonding, Invisalign and implants.
