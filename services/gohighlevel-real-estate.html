GoHighLevel systems for real estate agents and teams

GHL Certified Admin · One-time price, you own the build

I build GoHighLevel funnels, CRM pipelines and follow-up automations for real estate agents and teams. Buyer and seller leads are captured separately, routed to the right agent and nurtured until they're ready to talk.

What I build for agents and teams

  • Seller and buyer pages: a landing page, a home valuation or seller capture page, and a buyer page.
  • Instant follow-up: a text and email the moment a lead comes in, plus long-term nurture for leads who aren't ready yet.
  • Lead routing: round-robin assignment, lead scoring and alerts so hot leads reach an agent fast.
  • Pipeline stages for buyers and sellers so the team can see every deal.
  • GHL snapshots you can deploy again for new agents or locations.
