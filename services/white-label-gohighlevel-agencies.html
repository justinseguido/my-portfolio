Agencies (White Label)

White label GoHighLevel builds for agencies

HighLevel Certified Admin · Built under your brand

Short answer: I build and fix GoHighLevel accounts for marketing agencies, fully under your brand. You sell and own the client relationship. I build inside the client’s sub-account, test everything, hand it over with a Loom walkthrough and written notes, and your client never sees my name.

Most agencies I work with sell GoHighLevel well but don’t want a full time builder on payroll. I take the build and fix work off your plate, priced per job, so your margin stays predictable.

What I build for agencies

  • Workflows and automations: speed to lead, missed call text back, reminders, reactivation and nurture sequences.
  • Voice AI agents that answer calls and book into the calendar, with consent checks and SMS fallback.
  • Reputation and reviews: review requests with routing by star rating.
  • Integrations through Make.com, Zapier and webhooks, including Shopify, Klaviyo, Xero, Ascora, Dentally and Google Sheets.
  • IVR call routing and call boards.
  • Migrations from other CRMs into GoHighLevel, and reusable snapshots.
  • Account fixes and monthly health checks: workflows that stopped sending, SMS delivery problems, broken integrations and duplicate emails, found and fixed.

How we work together

01 You send the brief

What the client needs, which sub-account, and any wording or deadlines. Screenshots and voice notes are fine.

02 I quote a fixed price

Per job, agreed before work starts, so you can price your client with confidence. No surprise hours.

03 I build and test in-house

I work in the client’s sub-account through your agency invite and test with internal contacts first, so your client only sees a finished system.

04 Handover

A Loom walkthrough and written notes your team can follow, plus optional monthly maintenance per sub-account.

White label rules I follow

  • I never contact your clients unless you ask me to.
  • When you copy me into client emails, I write as part of your team.
  • I don’t mention GoHighLevel, my rates or other tools to your clients unless you want me to.
  • Your client names stay out of my portfolio. Case studies are anonymised, for example “a UK garage group”.

Proof: builds delivered for agency partners

  • MOT and service reminders for a UK garage group: 2,386 customers enrolled.
  • Shopify abandoned checkout texts for a UK e-commerce brand, connected with Klaviyo.
  • Xero to GoHighLevel review requests for a New Zealand service business, through Zapier.
